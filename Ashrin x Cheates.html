Buyer’s Guide

1. Before you buy: a checklist

Take two minutes to go through this list. First, decide what you actually want: a panel to try, or only diamonds. Second, check which account you plan to use and whether you can accept losing it. Third, check that your phone has enough storage and a stable internet connection. Fourth, read the plan list and pick the shortest one that lets you test the product properly. Fifth, make sure you have your name and phone number saved on the Dashboard, so your order message is complete. Sixth, keep WhatsApp open, because I will reply there. A calm, prepared buyer has a much smoother experience than someone who rushes.

2. Understanding durations and prices

Each product has several plans, from one hour up to thirty days. Short plans are cheap to try but expensive per hour. Long plans cost more at checkout but give the lowest cost per day. As an example, if one product costs Rs 450 for one day and Rs 2050 for thirty days, the month works out to roughly Rs 68 a day. That is a big saving if you really play every day, and a waste if you only play on weekends. Be honest with yourself about how often you play before choosing. Also note that the prices differ from product to product, so always compare the plans of the exact product you want.

3. How to avoid scams when buying game items

Fake sellers are common in gaming communities, so protect yourself. Be careful when a seller refuses to talk on a normal chat, pushes you to pay immediately, offers a price far below everyone else, or asks for your password or a code. Do not pay to unknown accounts that have no history. Keep screenshots of the chat and the payment. Check that you are on the official website and the official WhatsApp number shown here. If something feels wrong, stop and ask questions first. A real seller will answer calmly. This advice applies to every shop, including this one.

4. Protecting your account and your phone

Your account is worth protecting. Turn on every security option your game and email offer. Use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else. Never share verification codes. Download files only from the sender you trust, and read what a file asks to access before you install it. Keep your phone updated and think twice before installing anything from a random link. If your phone starts behaving strangely, such as unknown apps, heating or adverts, stop using the file and tell me. Also keep a backup of any important photos and contacts on your phone, as you would with any unofficial software.

5. Getting a smooth setup

Most setup problems are simple. Make sure your phone has free storage and a charged battery. Close extra apps so your phone has enough memory. Connect to a stable network, because a weak connection is the most common reason something fails to load. Read my instructions fully before you start, and follow them in order without skipping steps. If you get stuck, take a screenshot of exactly what you see and send it to me. A screenshot tells me far more than the words “it is not working”.

6. Troubleshooting common problems

If nothing happens after you follow the steps, restart your phone and try again once. If the game updated recently, tell me, because that is often the cause. If you see an error message, copy or screenshot it. If you were disconnected from the game, check your internet first. If a plan seems to end sooner than expected, send me your order details and the time you started. Please give me the facts, not only the frustration, and I will do what I can to solve it fairly.

Diamonds are the in-game currency used to buy characters, outfits and other items. In this store you can choose from 115, 240, 610 or 1240 diamonds. Larger packs give more diamonds for each rupee, so if you know you will spend them anyway, a bigger pack is better value. Check the amount twice in the Buy window before continuing. After payment, follow my instructions carefully, and never send passwords or login codes to anyone.

8. Playing fairly and responsibly

Free Fire is a team game, and other players are trying to enjoy it too. Third-party tools can make matches less fair for them, and that is one reason Garena forbids them. Think about this when you decide how you play. Beyond that, set limits on your spending and your screen time. Do not spend money meant for school, food or family needs. Games should be fun, and they are best when they stay balanced with studies, sleep and time with friends.
